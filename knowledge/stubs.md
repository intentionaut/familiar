# Stubs

The raw material of the publication: the one-line idea sent mid-flow, the
session that felt like a piece, the clip saved with `inspire`. Declared in
`docs/plans/content-strategy-bot.md`.

## Rules

**Append-only.** A stub is never edited in place, only triaged or retired.
Triage is a proposal, not a promotion: it names the theme, the audience,
the surface and the evidence a piece would need, and nothing moves without
the writer.

**Every entry carries its source**, per AGENTS.md, "Declared before
inferred". A stub in the writer's own words is `source: declared`, with
where it was said beside it.
