# Interview: ask up to three times, then change the angle

Status: tabled, not started. Raised 2026-08-31.

## The idea

`prompts/interview.md` says to chase specifics relentlessly and ask for the
scene behind an abstract answer. It does not say how many times. In practice
agents stop after two, decide the writer does not have the scene, and go
looking in the vault instead.

The rule should be: **ask up to three times, and make the third one a different
angle rather than the same question again.**

## The evidence for it

From the session that wrote `2026-08-30-familiar`:

- Q2 asked for one instance of a question-based interface helping her think.
  The answer named the lineage, no scene.
- Q3 asked for one time a Captain's Log question changed what she built. The
  answer described the kind of questions, no scene. The interviewer logged
  "Still no single scene after two asks; do not push a third time" and fell
  back to searching the vault.
- The piece then went all the way through outline, draft and dev-edit without
  the scene it needed, and drifted badly enough that the writer asked to
  restart from the first gate.
- On the return visit, the third ask came from a different angle: not "when did
  a question help you" but "when did you last lose something because you did
  not write it down". That produced Friday, the startup whose technical journey
  was never captured, which became the thesis of the whole piece.

Two asks was not enough. A third ask phrased the same way probably would not
have worked either. The angle change is the load-bearing part.
