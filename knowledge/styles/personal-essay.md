# Style: Personal essay

The default Familiar setup. A letter on your work: what you shipped, what you
learned, what it cost. One idea per issue, interview-first, no broetry.

## Positioning

```
Language: en
Tone: warm, specific, honest
Audience: people who do similar work and want to think better about it
Cadence: roughly fortnightly
```

## Voice

Write like you are talking to a smart colleague over coffee. First person.
Specific details over abstractions. No "excited to share" or "delved into".
The sentence habits in `voice-guide.md` are yours, not a template.

## Slot shapes

The pipeline produces one essay and a week of social posts:

- **Essay**: 1500-2500 words, interview-to-draft-to-two-edits
- **Social**: 5-7 posts, one per channel, each tested against `social-rules.md`
- **Repurpose (short)**: pull-quotes, statistics, and reframes from the essay
- **Repurpose (long)**: a companion piece that extends one thread from the essay

## What to change

- `positioning.md`: fill in your specific audience, not "everyone"
- `voice-guide.md`: describe your actual register, not a generic one
- `social-schedule.md`: pick your channels and cadence; the social stage
  refuses to guess
