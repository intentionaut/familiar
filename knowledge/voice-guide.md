# Voice guide

How you write. Familiar reads this before drafting or editing anything, and
treats `examples/canonical.md` as the proof. Fill it in from your own published
writing, not from how you would like to write. If you are unsure, paste three
pieces you are proud of into a session and ask Familiar to draft this file from
them, then correct what it gets wrong.

Until this file has real answers, the draft stage will ask for a sample rather
than guess.

## Register

Who is the piece written to, and from where?

- [one reader over coffee / a room / a peer you respect]
- [how early do personal stakes appear?]
- [direct address: you / your / the reader's name / never]
- [warmth level, and what counts as too much]

## Sentence craft

- [typical sentence length; whether fragments are allowed and when]
- [punctuation habits: commas, colons, semicolons, dashes]
- [rhythm: does it breathe, or stay clipped?]
- [reading ease target, if different from positioning.md]
- [lists: concrete nouns and real names, or abstractions?]

## Moves that make it yours

Four to six things a reader would recognise. Each one specific enough that an
editor could check for it.

1. [how you open]
2. [where the piece is grounded: a scene, a number, a claim]
3. [how you handle sources and quotes]
4. [coined terms: do you, and how are they marked?]
5. [what your headings sound like]
6. [how you end]

## Authority

How do you establish that you know what you are talking about? Most writers
lean on credentials; the stronger move is one precise scene, written as a
participant. Say which you want.

- [show / tell / a bit of both, and where the line is]
- [things you never do: résumé drops, "as a [title]", writing about others from above]

## Hard noes

The list the line edit will enforce without mercy. Add to it every time you
correct the same thing twice.

- [punctuation you never use]
- [words and phrases that make you wince]
- [structures you avoid: correlatives, hedging filler, marketing rhythm]
- [spellings]
- [anything that would not be said aloud to a friend]
