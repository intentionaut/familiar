Fill this in your own knowledge folder. These are windows for newsletters and
articles only, not social feed posts, replies or live engagement. The social
scheduler and its cadence remain in `knowledge/social-schedule.md`.

The writer may choose an exception for a particular piece. Record it with that
piece, not by quietly changing this standing table. An empty or placeholder row
is unconfigured: ask for a window rather than inventing a send time.

## Learning from engagement

For each published item, record its actual publication timestamp, channel,
format and observed engagement in the writer's private records. After a handful
of comparable posts, propose any change to these defaults with the evidence.
Do not update this table or move an accepted time without the writer's choice.
